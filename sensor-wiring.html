Soldering order

  1. Position without fixing: put the controller in its holder and hold the sensor near its final position, clear lens facing the ball. Work out wire lengths with a loose loop, not stretched tight. Remove the ball while soldering.
  2. Cut six insulated wires. Label both ends 1–6 with little pieces of tape. Strip only enough insulation for each joint; exposed wire must not touch neighbouring pins.
  3. Solder one wire at a time: follow the table. On the sensor, solder to its existing metal header pin; on the controller, solder into the labelled hole. Don't solder to the loose spare chip. Keep heat brief and allow joints to cool.
  4. Inspect before power: check every wire end against the table. Check for stray strands and solder joining adjacent pins. With the multimeter, check that VCC and GND are not shorted; a persistent near-zero resistance is a reason to stop.
  5. Send close photos of both wired boards before plugging in. Include readable labels and the power connections. We'll check them, flash the firmware, then test movement.
  6. Test before glue: temporarily hold the sensor in place without pressing its optics. Put the ball back, power the controller, and test cursor movement. Any claimed optical height remains provisional until this works.
